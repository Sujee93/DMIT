FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers to the questions we get asked most often.

Q. Are the courses available online or only in Colombo?

All our courses are available fully online, so you can learn from anywhere in Sri Lanka or abroad. We also offer in-person sessions in Colombo for students who prefer a classroom environment.

Q. Do I need prior experience to enrol?

Most of our courses are designed for complete beginners. We do offer intermediate and advanced tracks for those with existing knowledge — each course page clearly states the level required.

Q. Are DMIT certificates recognised by employers?

Yes. DMIT certificates are widely recognised across Sri Lanka and are verifiable digitally. We are also an authorised Google and Meta training partner, so qualifying courses come with internationally recognised credentials.

Q. Can I pay in instalments?

Absolutely. We offer flexible payment plans for all our courses. Get in touch with our team and we'll set up a plan that works for you.
