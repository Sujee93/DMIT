❓ FAQ

Common
Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions we receive from prospective students.

How do I enroll in a DMIT course? +

You can enroll by filling out the contact form on this page, calling us directly, or visiting our Colombo campus. Our admissions team will guide you through the registration process and help choose the right course for your goals.

Do you offer a free trial or demo class? +

Yes! We offer a free introductory session for all our main courses. You can attend one class online or in-person before making your enrollment decision. Contact us to book your free demo session today.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept cash, bank transfer, credit/debit cards, and online payment through our portal. We also offer flexible monthly payment plans for all courses. Contact our team to discuss the payment option that works best for you.

Can I switch courses after enrolling? +

Yes, within the first two weeks of enrollment, you can switch to a different course at no extra cost (subject to availability). After two weeks, a small administrative fee may apply. We want you to find the perfect course for your needs.
