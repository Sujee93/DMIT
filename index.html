❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Have questions about DMIT courses? We've answered the most common ones below. Feel free to contact us for anything else.

What courses does DMIT offer? +

DMIT offers a comprehensive range of digital marketing courses including SEO, Social Media Marketing, Google Ads (PPC), Email Marketing, Content Marketing, Web Development, Graphic Design, and Data Analytics. All courses are taught by industry professionals.

Are DMIT certificates recognized by employers? +

Yes! DMIT certifications are widely recognized by leading companies across Sri Lanka and internationally. Our courses align with Google, Meta, and HubSpot certification standards, giving you credentials that employers trust.

Can I study online or only in person? +

DMIT offers both online and in-person learning options. Our online courses are fully self-paced with lifetime access to video content, while in-person classes are held at our Colombo campus. Hybrid options are also available for select programs.

What is the duration of the courses? +

Course durations vary by program. Short courses typically run 4–8 weeks, while our professional diploma programs are 3–6 months. All courses include lifetime access to materials and regular content updates to keep you current with industry trends.

Is there a payment plan or scholarship available? +

Yes, DMIT offers flexible monthly payment plans for all courses. We also run scholarship programs for outstanding students and early-bird discounts for course registrations. Contact our admissions team to learn about the options available to you.
