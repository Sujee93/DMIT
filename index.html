FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Got a question about your dam? Here are the answers we hear most. Still unsure? Give us a call — we're happy to help.

Get a FREE Estimate 0466 315 944
How much does a dam cost to build?
Prices vary widely depending on the site conditions, size, and soil type. We'll provide a realistic estimate upfront, and often come in under.
Do you handle everything?
Yes. With all our machinery in-house — excavators, track loaders, rollers, trucks, and pumps — we manage the full job from site inspection through to final handover, with no reliance on hire companies.
Why do so many dams leak?
Most leaks come from poor preparation — skipping soil stripping, not keying the dam into natural ground, or inadequate compaction. Our technical approach prevents leaks where others fail.
Can you repair an old dam?
Absolutely. We specialise in fixing leaks and failures left by others. We'll assess your existing dam, give an honest overview of its condition, and recommend practical, cost-effective next steps.
Do you work with different soil types?
Yes. From volcanic red soils to yellow clays, we understand how different ground types impact dam design and longevity, and adjust our approach to suit your site.
