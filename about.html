About DMIT

Sri Lanka's leading Digital Marketing IT Institute — empowering professionals with world-class skills since 2018.

📖 Our Story

Built to Transform
Careers

DMIT was founded in 2018 with a simple mission: make world-class digital marketing education accessible to every Sri Lankan professional. What started as a small classroom in Colombo has grown into the country's most trusted digital marketing institute.

Our founder, a veteran digital marketing strategist with over 15 years of international experience, recognized a gap between industry needs and what was being taught in traditional academic settings. DMIT was born to bridge that gap.

Today, we have trained over 5,000 students who now work at Google, Meta, HubSpot, and leading agencies across Asia. Our curriculum is continuously updated to stay ahead of industry trends and algorithm changes.
