Our Story

Where Digital Ambition Meets Expert Guidance

Founded in 2015 in the heart of Colombo, DMIT was born from a simple frustration: Sri Lankan professionals deserved access to world-class digital marketing education — not outdated textbooks, not generic webinars, but practical, industry-driven training.

Our founders — digital marketers and technologists with decades of combined experience — built DMIT to bridge that gap. Today, with over 5,000 graduates and 25+ specialised courses, we're proud to be the institute where Sri Lanka's top digital talent begins their journey.

Curriculum designed and reviewed by active industry professionals
Internationally recognised certification programmes
Real-world projects with live brands and campaigns
Dedicated career support and alumni network

Our Journey

Key Milestones

From a small classroom in Colombo to Sri Lanka's leading digital institute.

2015

DMIT Founded

Launched with 3 courses and 50 students in Colombo 3, with a vision to redefine digital education in Sri Lanka.

2017

1,000 Students Milestone

Crossed 1,000 enrolled students and expanded the course catalogue to 10 specialised programmes.

2019

Online Platform Launch

Launched our fully online learning platform, enabling students from across Sri Lanka to learn from anywhere.

2021

International Recognition

Partnered with Google and Meta for certified training programmes, becoming an authorised training partner.
