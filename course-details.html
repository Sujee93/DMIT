Course Description

The Complete Digital Marketing Masterclass is DMIT's flagship course, designed to take you from absolute beginner to confident digital marketing professional. Whether you're a business owner, career changer, or marketing professional looking to upskill, this course covers everything you need.

With over 24 hours of on-demand video content, real-world projects, and live Q&A sessions with our expert instructors, you'll gain the practical skills that Sri Lankan businesses are actively looking for. Our curriculum is updated every quarter to stay ahead of Google, Meta, and industry algorithm changes.

Upon completion, you'll receive the DMIT Professional Certificate in Digital Marketing, recognized by over 200 companies across Sri Lanka and the region.
