From The Founder

Why I Started ZelClean

After nearly a decade in commercial cleaning, I'd seen the same story repeat: large companies winning contracts with polished proposals, then handing the work off to untrained subcontractors who didn't care.

Business owners would call to complain — and get transferred to three different people who all said the same thing: "We'll look into it." Nobody fixed it. Nobody was accountable.

I knew there was a better way. When you work with ZelClean, you're dealing directly with me from day one. I do the site visit. I build your cleaning plan. I train the team that works in your building. And if something ever isn't right, you call me directly — not a ticketing system.

That level of personal accountability is rare in this industry. It's exactly why we have a 98% client retention rate, and it's something I'm genuinely proud of.

— Zeljan

Founder & Director, ZelClean Melbourne
