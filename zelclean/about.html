From The Founder

Why I Started ZelClean

After nearly a decade in the commercial cleaning industry, I'd seen the same story repeat itself: large companies winning contracts with polished proposals, then handing the work off to untrained subcontractors who didn't care.

Business owners would call to complain — and get transferred to three different people who all said the same thing: "We'll look into it." Nobody fixed it. Nobody was accountable.

I knew there was a better way. When you work with ZelClean, you're dealing directly with me from day one. I do the site visit. I build your cleaning plan. I train the team that works in your building. And if something ever isn't right, you call me — not a ticketing system.

That level of personal accountability is rare in this industry. It's exactly why we have a 98% client retention rate, and it's something I'm genuinely proud of.

— Zeljan

Founder & Director, ZelClean Melbourne

Our Values Drive Everything We Do

These aren't words on a wall. They're the principles Zeljan built ZelClean on — and they show up in every clean.

🤝

Accountability

We own our mistakes and fix them fast. Every issue gets a direct response from the owner — not a promise that "someone will follow up."

📋

Consistency

Your office should look the same every single visit. We use detailed site-specific checklists and the same dedicated team on every job.

Clear Communication

We tell you what we're doing, when we're doing it, and flag anything that needs your attention. No guessing games, no silence.

Professionalism
