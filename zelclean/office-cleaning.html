What's Included in Your Clean

Our standard office clean covers everything — we don't do "basic" packages with hidden add-ons.

🖥️

Workspaces & Offices

  • Desk surfaces & monitors wiped down
  • Keyboards & high-touch point sanitisation
  • Internal glass & partitions cleaned
  • Chairs & fabric surfaces maintained
  • Bin liners replaced
  • Floors vacuumed & mopped
  • Skirting boards & ledges dusted

Kitchen & Amenities

  • Benchtops & splashbacks cleaned
  • Sink scrubbed & sanitised
  • Microwave interior & exterior
  • Fridge exteriors wiped down

  • Cupboard fronts wiped

Bathrooms & Washrooms

  • Toilets fully scrubbed & sanitised

  • Floors mopped with disinfectant
  • Consumables restocked (soap, paper)

  • Exhaust fans dusted

Reception & Common Areas
